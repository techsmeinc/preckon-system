The modules

Six intelligences, one chain.

Each module reads one kind of document and hands structured output to the next — from a raw tender package all the way to a procurement package. Here's what each one actually does.

Module 01 / 06 · Tender Intelligence

TenderLogix

Turn a tender package into a structured brief.

TenderLogix reads the full tender — drawings register, contract, scope and instructions to bidders — and pulls out what actually governs the job. Nothing important stays buried on page 240.

  • ✓ Extracts requirements and obligations into a traceable register
  • ✓ Flags mandatory clauses, submittals and deadlines
  • ✓ Structures the project so every later layer has context

Module 02 / 06 · Drawing Intelligence

DrawLogix

Read drawings the way an estimator does — every sheet, every time.

DrawLogix interprets architectural and structural drawings in PDF or CAD, recognizes building elements and measures their geometry. Walls, columns, slabs, openings — identified, dimensioned, ready to quantify.

  • ✓ Recognizes elements across PDF and CAD sheets
  • ✓ Measures geometry and dimensions automatically
  • ✓ Links every measurement back to the sheet it came from

Module 03 / 06 · Specification Intelligence

DocLogix

Tie every quantity to the clause that governs it.

Specs decide what a line item actually is — the grade, the finish, the standard. DocLogix parses the specification and connects each clause to the elements and items it applies to, so nothing is priced generically.

  • ✓ Parses specification clauses and standards
  • ✓ Maps clauses to elements and BOQ items
  • ✓ Keeps attributes — grade, finish, standard — attached to quantities

Module 04 / 06 · Quantity Intelligence

QuantLogix

A structured BOQ, generated — not rebuilt by hand.

QuantLogix turns measured elements and matched specs into a structured bill of quantities and takeoff. Line items, quantities, units — assembled to your method of measurement, ready to cost.

  • ✓ Generates the BOQ and takeoff from elements + specs
  • ✓ Follows your standard method of measurement
  • ✓ Every quantity traceable to its source element

Module 05 / 06 · Cost Intelligence

CostLogix

A costed, defensible estimate you can trace and adjust.

CostLogix applies rates to every line and builds the estimate. Rates you can see, source and override — with the total updating live and every number traceable back through the BOQ to the drawing.

  • ✓ Applies and manages rates across the BOQ
  • ✓ Builds a costed estimate with live totals
  • ✓ Traceable from total → line → element → drawing
