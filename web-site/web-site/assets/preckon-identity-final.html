06 / RULES

Do & don’t

Do

  • Keep teal on the reckoning point only — the node, and the o in the wordmark.
  • Reverse to paper on dark; keep the node teal.
  • Give it the full clear space; use mono navy when one color is required.

Don’t

  • Don’t make the “k” teal, or color more than one letter.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows/gradients to the mark.
  • Don’t recolor the stem or node outside the approved set.
