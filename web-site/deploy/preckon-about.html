About Preckon

Rebuilding the front of construction.

The numbers a project is won or lost on are still produced by hand, slowly, and locked in a few people's heads. We're changing that — making preconstruction fast, defensible, and open to everyone who prices the work.

Mission

Turn documents into decisions.

Give every contractor and quantity surveyor a way to go from a raw tender package to a costed, defensible estimate in hours — with the reasoning shown and a person in control at every step.

Vision

Preconstruction, no longer the bottleneck.

A world where bid throughput isn't limited by how many senior estimators you can hire, where no number is a black box, and where the knowledge to price a project isn't trapped in one person's spreadsheets.

Why we built it

Estimating is the hardest, slowest, least-shared part of the job.

Before a shovel touches the ground, someone has to read the drawings, measure everything, decide what it's made of, price it, and package it for buyout. It's the work that decides whether a bid wins and whether a project makes money — and it's still done largely by hand, in spreadsheets, under a clock.

The most consequential numbers in construction are produced the slowest, by the fewest people, with the least ability to audit them.

Takeoff tools measure. Estimating tools price, manually. Project platforms manage what already exists. Nobody carried the whole thing — from raw documents to a procurement package — in one place, with the reasoning attached. So we built Preckon to do exactly that: read the documents, generate the chain, and keep the estimator's judgment at the center of it.

How we build

Four principles under the hood.

AI-native

Built around AI from the first line — not a chatbot bolted onto a document viewer.

Provider-independent

No single AI vendor to lock us — or your data — in. We route to the right model for each task.

Explainable & reviewed

Every output traces to its source and passes through a human. Nothing auto-commits.

Grounded in construction

A real construction knowledge graph, so the platform reasons about buildings — not generic text.
