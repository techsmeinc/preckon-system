02 / LOGOMARK

Three routes for the mark

Same brand language, three levels of literalness — from a lettered “P” to a fully abstract measurement glyph. All work in one flat navy + one teal accent.

Set-Out P
A drafted “P” with the reckoning node in its counter. Ownable letterform, reads instantly.
RECOMMENDED
Datum Circle
The house icon-in-circle: a line stepping up to a node. Best for avatars & favicons.
Ascending Node
Fully abstract: quantities rising over a dimension line to a value. Most “platform”.
