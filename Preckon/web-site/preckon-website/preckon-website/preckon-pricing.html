Pricing questions

Before you ask sales.

How much does it actually cost?
Cost has two parts — a platform plan plus usage-based AI. We're finalizing public rates with our first customers, so the fastest way to a real number is a short demo where we size it to your bid volume. Design partners get preferred early terms.
How does usage-based billing work?
You're metered per unit of work the platform produces — drawings analyzed, BOQs generated, estimates produced, procurement packages. Cost tracks output, so a quiet month costs less and a busy bid season scales with the work.
What's included in Enterprise?
Everything in Professional plus SSO and advanced RBAC, private deployment and regional data residency, per-tenant white-labeling, dedicated onboarding, and an SLA — on an annual agreement.
Can I try it before committing?
Yes — bring one drawing set to a demo and watch it run end to end on your own project. The Design Partner Program is the fastest route to running real bids with the team alongside you.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes. Teams can bill annually, and Enterprise runs on a custom annual agreement.
