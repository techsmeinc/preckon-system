========================================================================
DrawLogix — Solutions Map
"Don't start from a blank page. Get a usable drawing, finish it in AutoCAD."
========================================================================

WHO IT'S FOR
------------
AutoCAD drafters who don't want to start from scratch. DrawLogix does the
tedious, repetitive setup, hands over a solid DXF, and they open it in
AutoCAD to make the major design decisions and modifications.

THE RULE EVERY FEATURE MUST PASS
--------------------------------
  (a) It kills a time sink, AND
  (b) It hands off cleanly to AutoCAD.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FOUR SOLUTIONS  (manual pain  ->  DrawLogix  ->  AutoCAD)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. BLANK-PAGE SETUP  ->  FIRST-PASS PLAN                    [BUILT]
   Pain:   Laying out walls, rooms, doors, corridors from a brief/SOW.
   Does:   Reads the SOW and generates a dimensioned, layered DXF
           (walls, doors, windows, room labels, corridor, title block).
   Result: Drafter opens it in AutoCAD with ~70% of the linework done
           and just adjusts the layout. Saves hours of initial drafting.

2. MANUAL MEASURING / COUNTING  ->  INSTANT TAKEOFF + BOM   [BUILT]
   Pain:   Measuring every room area, counting doors/windows, tallying
           wall lengths, pricing.
   Does:   Upload an AutoCAD DXF -> auto quantities + priced BOM + CSV
           (with unit auto-detect).
   Result: Removes the whole manual quantity-survey step.

3. REPETITIVE CLEANUP / STANDARDIZATION  ->  AI COPILOT     [BUILT]
   Pain:   Renaming layers to office standards, batch find-replace on
           labels, recolouring, adding title blocks / revision notes.
   Does:   Upload DXF, instruct in plain English, export DXF.
   Result: Removes the tedious drawing-management chores.

4. MANUAL ANNOTATION  ->  AUTO-LABELING                     [NEXT]
   Pain:   Labeling every room with its name + area and adding dimension
           strings — one of the most tedious repetitive AutoCAD tasks.
   Does:   Upload a plan with rooms -> detects each space and writes the
           area/name tags + dimension strings onto proper layers ->
           export the annotated DXF.
   Result: A fully-tagged drawing to review, not a blank one.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE AUTOCAD-NATIVE HANDOFF  (foundation under all four)     [DELIVERED]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Output is genuinely AutoCAD-ready so the "then modify" step is frictionless:

  - Standard AIA layers:
      A-WALL, A-AREA, A-DOOR, A-GLAZ, A-ANNO, A-DIMS, A-TTLB
  - Rooms as CLOSED POLYLINES on A-AREA
      -> AutoCAD AREA / HATCH / room-tag tools work immediately
  - Door swings (leaf + arc) on A-DOOR
  - Room tags, dimension strings, title block
  - Units set to metres ($INSUNITS)

Verified round-trip (buildDxf -> takeoff):
  layers: A-WALL, A-AREA, A-DOOR, A-GLAZ, A-ANNO, A-DIMS, A-TTLB
  spaces detected reliably: RECEPTION=24, OFFICE 1=30, OFFICE 2=30,
                            WC=6, CIRCULATION=35  (floor 125 m2)


------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT MATTERS FOR THE CLIENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Give a brief (or upload a rough DXF) -> get a layered, dimensioned,
area-tagged DXF where AutoCAD's own tools already work -> spend time on
the major parts and modifications, not on setup, labeling, or measuring.

  => 60-70% time saving on the drudge work.
